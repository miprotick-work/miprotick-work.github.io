This Privacy Policy explains how **Hidayah** ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Hidayah is an Android app, developed by **Mezbaul Islam Protick**, that shows verses of the Quran as a wallpaper and a home-screen widget.

> **In short:** Hidayah does **not** collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. We have no servers that hold user data, and the App contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

Some information is saved **only on your device**, in the App's private storage, so that the App works the way you set it up:

- **Your settings:** translation language, verse length, design, colours, the Arabic text on or off, and the update schedules for the wallpaper and widget.
- **Recently shown verses:** so the App can display and refresh them.

This information is never sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google account backup, under Google's own policies. You can delete it at any time; see [Data Deletion](/hidayah/delete-account/).

## Images you save or copy

When you tap **Download image**, the App saves the preview image to your device's gallery (*Pictures/Hidayah*). On Android 8 and 9 it saves to a location you choose. When you tap **Copy image**, a temporary copy is written to the App's private cache so it can be placed on your clipboard; only the most recent copy is kept. These images stay on your device, and we never receive them.

## Network requests

To show a new verse, the App requests it from the free **AlQuran Cloud** service (`api.alquran.cloud`) over an encrypted **HTTPS** connection. The request contains only:

- a verse number, and
- the editions to return (Arabic text and the translations you chose).

It contains **no personal information**. As with any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. AlQuran Cloud's handling of that is governed by its own policies: <https://alquran.cloud>.

The App makes no other network requests.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | To download verses from AlQuran Cloud |
| Set wallpaper | To apply the wallpaper you choose to your home and/or lock screen |
| Set wallpaper hints | To size the wallpaper correctly for your screen |

The App does **not** access your contacts, location, photos, files, camera, microphone, or any other personal data. Saving an image doesn't need storage permission: Android 10+ writes it directly to your gallery, and on Android 8–9 you pick the location yourself.

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AlQuran Cloud](https://alquran.cloud) | Provides the Quran verses and translations | Verse number and edition names only (plus the IP address that every internet request reveals) |

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal information, because we don't collect any.

## Data security

All communication with AlQuran Cloud is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Data stored on your device is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All the App's data is on your device and under your control. If you have any questions, email {{contact}}.
