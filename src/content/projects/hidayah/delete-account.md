**Hidayah does not have user accounts** and does not store any of your data on a server. There is no account to delete, and we hold nothing about you that we could delete on your behalf.

Everything the App saves stays **on your device**, and you can remove it yourself at any time.

## What Hidayah stores on your device

| Data | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| Settings (translation, verse length, design, colours, schedules) | The App's private storage |
| Recently shown verses | The App's private storage |
| The most recent copied image | The App's private cache |
| Images you chose to download | Your gallery, in *Pictures/Hidayah* |

## Delete the App's data

**Option 1: Clear storage** (keeps the App installed and resets it)

1. Open *Settings → Apps → Hidayah*.
2. Tap **Storage & cache**.
3. Tap **Clear storage**, then **Clear cache**.

**Option 2: Uninstall the App**

Uninstalling Hidayah removes all of its settings, verses and cached files from your device.

## Delete images you downloaded

Images you saved with **Download image** belong to you and are **not** removed when you uninstall the App. To delete them, open your gallery or Files app, go to *Pictures/Hidayah*, and delete the images or the whole folder.

## Device backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android may have included Hidayah's settings in your personal Google account backup. Google manages these backups, and you can delete them in your Google account's backup settings.
