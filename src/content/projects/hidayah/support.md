Need help with **Hidayah**? Start with the answers below. If they don't help, we're happy to help by email.

## Contact support

Email {{contact}}. We usually reply within **2 business days**.

To help us solve your problem faster, please include:

- your phone model and Android version;
- the Hidayah version (*Settings → Apps → Hidayah*, at the bottom of the page);
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if it helps.

## Frequently asked questions

### My wallpaper or widget doesn't update automatically

Android schedules background work, and its shortest interval is **15 minutes**. Updates can be delayed when battery saver is on or when the phone is idle. Some manufacturers, including **Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Samsung**, also restrict background apps aggressively. To fix it:

1. Open *Settings → Apps → Hidayah → Battery*.
2. Choose **Unrestricted**, or turn off battery optimisation for Hidayah.
3. On Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo phones, also allow **Autostart** for Hidayah.

### How do I add the widget?

Open Hidayah and tap **Add to home**. You can also long-press your home screen, choose **Widgets**, find **Hidayah** and drag it onto the home screen. The widget is resizable, from about 1 to 3 rows tall.

### Can the wallpaper and widget show different verses?

Yes. They are completely independent, each with its own verse, look and schedule. You can use either one on its own.

### How do I try a new verse before applying it?

Tap **Update preview** to see a new verse and design. When you like it, tap **Update wallpaper now** to apply it to the home screen, lock screen or both.

### How do I change the translation or hide the Arabic text?

In the App, choose **English**, **Bangla**, or both, and switch the Arabic text on or off. The wallpaper and the widget each have their own settings for this.

### It says "No internet" or "Server unavailable"

Hidayah needs an internet connection to fetch a new verse. Check your connection and tap **Retry**. If the verse service is temporarily down, automatic updates will quietly try again later.

### How do I save or share a verse image?

On the wallpaper or widget preview, tap **Download image** to save it to your gallery (*Pictures/Hidayah*), or **Copy image** to paste it into another app.

### Does Hidayah collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](/hidayah/privacy-policy/).

## Reporting a mistake in a verse or translation

Verses and translations come from [AlQuran Cloud](https://alquran.cloud). If you notice an error, email us with the verse reference (surah and ayah number) shown on the wallpaper, and we'll look into it.

## Reporting a bug or security issue

Email {{contact}} with the subject line "Bug" or "Security". Please report security issues privately rather than publicly.
