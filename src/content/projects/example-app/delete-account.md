You can delete your **Example App** account and its associated data at any time.

## Option 1: Delete from inside the app

1. Open **Example App** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account**. <!-- TODO: confirm the path -->
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deactivated immediately.

## Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to **{{contact}}** from the email address linked to your account, with the subject **"Account deletion request"**. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before we delete anything.

We will process your request within **30 days** and confirm by email when it's done.

## What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Account profile (name, email) | Permanently deleted |
| Content you created in the app | Permanently deleted |
| Usage and diagnostics data linked to your account | Deleted or irreversibly anonymised |

## What may be retained

Some information may be kept for up to **90 days** in encrypted backups before it is permanently overwritten. We may also keep limited records, such as purchase transaction records, when the law requires it or to prevent fraud. <!-- TODO: adjust to reality -->

> Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account. Use one of the options above.

Questions? Email **{{contact}}**. See also our [Privacy Policy](/example-app/privacy-policy/).
